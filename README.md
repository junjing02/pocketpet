# PocketPet

A tiny pixel-dot virtual pet that lives in your browser — feed it, play with it, watch it grow, and pick up the same pet from any device.

**Play:** https://junjing02.github.io/pocketpet/app.html

## How to play

Hatch an egg and raise it through `hatchling → young → teen → juvenile → adult` — every stage is a distinct hand-drawn pixel silhouette, not just a bigger copy of the last. Which creature you get is a surprise, revealed only once it hatches.

- **Care**: keep Hunger, Happy, Energy, and Clean up via Feed (Snack or Meal), Play, Clean, and Sleep. Health drops from neglect and recovers on its own once you're back on top of things — unless it bottoms out into sick, which needs Medicine.
- **Playground**: tap your pet to poke it, tap empty space to send it walking there, or drag it around directly.
- **Economy**: earn coins by playing one of four mini-games (Tap the Target, Stop the Marker, Odd One Out, Count the Dots), then spend them on food or a cosmetic Bow.
- **Achievements**: Fully Grown, Coin Collector, Never Sick, Pristine Care — computed live from how you've actually played.
- **Care-quality reward**: raise your pet well and the adult form gets a small sparkle no neglected pet will ever show.
- **Daily streak**: log in on consecutive days for a growing coin bonus.
- **Multiple pets**: hatch up to 3 at once and switch between them — release one to free up a slot for a new surprise egg.
- **Cross-device**: sign in anywhere and it's the same pet.
