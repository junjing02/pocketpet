# PocketPet

A tiny pixel-dot virtual pet that lives in your browser. Feed it, play with it, watch it grow, and pick it up again from any device.

**Play:** https://junjing02.github.io/pocketpet/app.html

## How to play

Hatch an egg and raise it from hatchling to adult. Every stage looks different, and you won't know what creature you got until it hatches.

- **Care**: feed, play, clean, and let it sleep to keep its stats up. Neglect it too long and it gets sick, which needs medicine to fix.
- **Playground**: tap your pet to poke it, tap elsewhere to call it over, or just drag it around.
- **Mini-games**: play one of four quick games to earn coins, then spend them on food or a cosmetic bow.
- **Achievements**: unlocked automatically based on how well you've raised your pet.
- **Daily streak**: log in on consecutive days for a bigger coin bonus.
- **Multiple pets**: raise up to 3 at once, and release one anytime to make room for a new surprise egg.
- **Cross-device**: sign in anywhere to pick up the same pet.
