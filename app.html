How to Play

Growing upHatch a surprise egg and raise it through 6 stages. Health 50+ is needed to evolve.
CareFeed, Play, Clean, and Sleep keep your pet's stats up. Neglect drops Health, and hitting 0 makes it sick (needs Medicine).
Needs a handLow stats show right on the pet: shaking, itching, drooping, or too tired to move, so you don't have to watch the bars.
PlaygroundTap to poke it, tap elsewhere to call it over, or drag it around.
EconomyPlay mini-games for coins. Spend them in the Shop on food, a cosmetic Bow, and upgrades that help your pet recover faster.
Multiple petsHatch up to 3 from the ⇄ icon. Switch, rename, reset, or release one there.
Daily bonusLog in daily for a growing coin bonus.
