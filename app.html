How to Play

EggJust needs time to hatch — no feeding, playing, or sleeping yet.
CareFeed, Play, and Clean keep Hunger, Happy, and Clean above 0. Sleep restores Energy.
HealthDrops only from neglect, and recovers on its own once every stat is above 0 again.
SickHealth hitting 0 makes your pet sick — that needs Medicine, care alone won't cure it.
Needs a handAny stat at 20 or below shows on the pet itself — shaking when hungry, drooping when sad, itching when dirty, nodding off when tired — so you don't have to watch the bars.
EconomyFood is limited. Play opens a mini-game that earns coins — spend them on Snacks, Meals, or a cosmetic Bow (no stat effect, just looks nice).
Growing upHealth 50+ and enough time passed evolves: egg → hatchling → young → teen → juvenile → adult — each stage looks completely different.
Surprise eggEvery egg looks the same — you won't know which creature you got until it hatches.
Daily bonusLog in on consecutive days for a growing coin bonus, shown in the away-time recap.
Multiple petsHatch up to 3 pets and switch between them with the ⇄ icon at the top.
SettingsRename your pet, view Achievements, or enable low-stat notifications from the gear icon.
