How to Play

CareFeed, Play, and Clean keep Hunger, Happy, and Clean above 0. Sleep restores Energy.
HealthDrops only from neglect, and recovers on its own once every stat is above 0 again.
SickHealth hitting 0 makes your pet sick — that needs Medicine, care alone won't cure it.
EconomyFood is limited. Play doubles as a mini-game that earns coins — spend them with Buy Food.
Growing upHealth 50+ and enough time passed evolves: egg → baby → child → teen → adult.
